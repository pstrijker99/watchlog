Your services are highlighted on every title, and Discover can show only what you can stream. Data by JustWatch.

Saves your whole log to a private gist on your GitHub account after every change. On a new phone, install the app, paste the same token and tap Restore. Create the token at github.com → Settings → Tokens: tick only “gist” and set expiration to “No expiration”.

This deletes everything Watchlog keeps on this device: your log, lists, thoughts, settings and any keys or tokens you entered. It can’t be undone.
